AvaVideoPlayer/Playback

A view in every direction

Use a full 360 × 180° equirectangular panorama, then move the camera inside it.

Select the projection

Player.SetProjection(VideoProjection.Equirectangular);
Player.SetCamera(new OrbitCamera(Yaw: 45, Pitch: 0, FieldOfView: 80));

Use VideoProjection.Flat for ordinary video. An equirectangular source commonly has a 2:1 aspect ratio. Aspect ratio alone cannot establish that a video is spherical; select the correct mode for the content.

Mouse and touch

A centered wireframe sphere and 360° label invite you to click the video to capture the mouse. Move the mouse to orbit; double-click or press Escape to release capture and show the prompt again. Scroll to zoom while captured. Touch uses a direct drag gesture. Switching to flat video, replacing the source, or leaving the window releases capture. Browser mouse orbit uses Pointer Lock inside the media viewport. You can also use SetCamera. Camera yaw wraps, pitch is clamped to −89…89 degrees and field of view to 30…110 degrees. ResetCamera returns the control to the default camera.

Stereo layouts

Player.SetProjection(VideoProjection.Equirectangular, StereoLayout.TopBottom);

Mono, TopBottom and SideBySide describe source packing. The renderer selects the appropriate eye region. This is not a claim of headset or stereoscopic display certification.

Try real footage

The live demo includes a waterfall in Northern Ireland and a village view in Esino Lario, Italy, with captured location sound. Each recording appears once. Container/codec variants are selected with the Format control; the View control switches Flat/360° without duplicating catalog entries. An explicit 360°/Flat selector also lets you choose the view for a local file.
